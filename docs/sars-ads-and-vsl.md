## 1. What the winning ad does, and what to keep

The "SARS Penalties" video is 24 seconds and follows one pattern:

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0 to 5s | Presenter to camera, holding papers. Big title at the top: "Do YOU get late penalty fine from SARS" with red double-exclamation icons either side. |
| 5 to 12s | B-roll while the voice gives the number: "R250 up until R16,000 every month until you file." |
| 12 to 15s | Presenter: "The good news, the sooner you deal with it, the more options you have." |
| 15 to 20s | B-roll of a team: "BizXcel is a registered SARS tax practitioner. We get you back up to date." |
| 20 to 24s | Presenter, CTA title at the top: "Book YOUR FREE consultation down below." |

Captions are burned in, one or two words at a time, with the key word in yellow.

**Keep:** the presenter, the set (shelves behind, black shirt, lapel mic held in hand), the fear-then-relief order, the big top title, the yellow keyword captions, and the length (22 to 28 seconds).

**Fix in the new ads:**

1. **The top title gets cropped in Feed.** Feed shows a vertical video at 4:5, which cuts about 285px off the top and bottom of a 1080 x 1920 frame. The current title sits at the very top, so Feed viewers never see it. In the new ads the title sits between **y 300 and y 440**. That's inside the Feed crop and below the Reels profile row (top 250px).
2. **Captions** sit between **y 1300 and y 1500**. That's above the Reels caption and button area (bottom 420px) and inside the Feed crop.
3. **No foreign money.** The old B-roll shows US dollar notes. South Africans notice. Use rand, a calendar, or BizXcel's real office.
4. **Real people, not stock.** The team B-roll in the old ad is a stock photo. Film the real BizXcel team for 5 seconds (see the shot list). It's also the proof that this is a real local firm.
5. **The CTA changes.** The ads now send people to the page with the video, so the line is "Tap below and watch the short video", not "Book a free consultation".

## 2. Ad 1: "The meter"

**Angle:** the penalty is a meter that keeps running. Only uses figures SARS publishes itself, so it can run for months.
**Who it's for:** anyone with a return outstanding, whatever kind of taxpayer they are.
**Run:** from launch, no end date.

**Opening frame text (0.0 to 2.5s):** `Is SARS charging you every month?` with the red double-exclamation icons either side, same style as the original.

**Full spoken script (about 26 seconds):**

> Got a tax return that's still outstanding? Here's what nobody tells you.
> SARS can charge you a penalty for every month it's late. Anywhere from two hundred and fifty to sixteen thousand rand a month.
> Even the lowest one, two hundred and fifty rand, is three thousand rand a year. And it can keep going for up to thirty-five months.
> The good news? It stops once the return is filed.
> BizXcel is a registered SARS tax practitioner. We get you back up to date.
> Tap below and watch the short video.

**A-roll notes:**
- Same set, shirt and mic as the original, so the account's best-performing look carries over.
- Say the numbers slowly. They're the reason people stop.
- Beat 4 is the turn from worry to relief. Smile on "good news".

**B-roll list:**
1. A desk calendar, a hand flipping the pages one month at a time (8 seconds).
2. A plain, unopened envelope on a desk. No SARS logo, no fake letter.
3. The BizXcel team at their desks, a wide shot from the doorway (with their OK).
4. Wayne at his desk, looking at a screen turned away from camera.

**Cutaway that can't be filmed (the meter, beat 3):** build it in the motion studio. A simple rand counter ticking up month by month, navy #13293D background, gold #B4813C numbers, Lora for the figures.
- Fallback still (Gemini): *Create a vertical 9:16 ad graphic. Subject: a close-up of a desk calendar on a wooden desk, pages mid-flip, a pen beside it. Style: clean and modern. Colour palette: navy #13293D, warm wood, gold #B4813C. Composition: leave clear negative space in the centre for a number overlay. Lighting: soft and natural. Mood: calm, slightly serious. Avoid: clutter, any embedded text, any words, any logos, any readable interface text, any currency.*

- Stock fallback: Pexels "calendar pages flipping".

**3-question filter:**
- Understood in 3 seconds? Yes: "SARS might be charging me every month."
- One clear next step? Yes: watch the video.
- Intentional, not chaotic? Yes: one title or one number on screen at a time.

## 3. Ad 2: "Side income, 22 January"

**Angle:** a real date straight after the holidays. Reaches people who don't think of themselves as "behind with SARS" yet.
**Who it's for:** provisional taxpayers: people with rent, a side business or freelance income.
**Run:** 1 December 2026 to 22 January 2027, then switch it off.

**Opening frame text (0.0 to 2.5s):** `Side income? Read this.` with the red double-exclamation icons either side.

**Full spoken script (about 25 seconds):**

> Do you earn money outside your salary? Rent, a side business, freelance work?
> Then you're probably a provisional taxpayer. And your tax return is due on the twenty-second of January.
> That's straight after the holidays. Miss it, and SARS can charge a penalty every month until it's in.
> So sort it before the holidays, and January stays quiet.
> BizXcel is a registered SARS tax practitioner. We'll get it done properly and on time.
> Tap below and watch the short video.

**Confirm before filming:**
- **"Probably a provisional taxpayer"** stays as "probably". Most people with non-salary income are, but not all.
- **"Sort it before the holidays":** check BizXcel's December closing dates. If the office closes early, film "So sort it now, and January stays quiet" as a second take.

**A-roll notes:** lighter energy than Ad 1. This is helpful, not a warning. Film it on the same day as Ad 1, same set.

**B-roll list:**
1. A desk calendar turned to January with 22 circled (or build it in the motion studio).
2. A packed beach bag or cooler box beside a closed laptop.
3. The BizXcel team (shared with Ad 1).
4. A team member on the phone, taking notes.

**Cutaway that can't be filmed (the holiday shot):**
- Gemini still: *Create a vertical 9:16 ad graphic. Subject: a packed beach bag, sunglasses and a cooler box on a wooden floor, next to a closed laptop and a neat folder. Style: warm and human. Colour palette: warm summer light, navy #13293D, gold #B4813C. Composition: leave clear negative space in the top third for text overlay. Lighting: bright and airy. Mood: relaxed, holiday is close. Avoid: clutter, any embedded text, any words, any logos, any readable interface text.*
- Google Flow, 8 seconds: *A hand places sunglasses on the beach bag, then the camera slowly pushes in toward the closed laptop. Calm, warm light, no text.*
- Stock fallback: Pexels "beach bag packed" or "summer holiday packing".

**3-question filter:**
- Understood in 3 seconds? Yes: "If I have side income, this is about me."
- One clear next step? Yes: watch the video.
- Intentional, not chaotic? Yes.

**Visual difference between the two:** Ad 1 opens on a worried prop (the envelope) and leans on numbers. Ad 2 opens on a gesture (counting on fingers) and leans on a date and the holidays. Different first frames give Meta two distinct ads to learn from.

## 5. Campaign setup

- **One campaign, one ad set, three ads:** Ad 1, the original "SARS Penalties" video (re-pointed to the page), and from 1 December, Ad 2. The original stays in because it's proven. It shows whether the page beats the Instant Form with the same creative.
- **Keep the Instant Form campaign running** alongside it, at the same budget, until the page wins on **cost per booked consultation**, not cost per lead. Cost per lead will be higher on the page. That's expected.
- **Optimise for Landing Page Views in week 1.** The new dataset has no history. Keep Lead firing the whole time, and switch to Lead once it records 15 to 20 a week.
- **Targeting:** broad, South Africa, ages 25 to 60. Facebook Feed and Instagram Feed first.
- **Budget:** agree it with Wayne before launch. Run at least 7 to 14 days before judging.
- **Hooks from VSL B:** once the VSL is live, cut the hook test ads from it (VSL B doc, "How we test the hooks"). Three at a time, in the same campaign.
- **Don't edit a running ad.** Duplicate it and change the copy, or the results reset.

## 6. VSL edit plan (footage already filmed)

**Where it goes:** the top of `/sars`, in a 4:5 player.

**Export spec:**
- **4:5, 1080 x 1350.** The footage is portrait. Crop from the 9:16 frame, keeping the head in the upper third and room for captions below the chest.
- 2:30 to 3:00 maximum. If it runs longer, cut the "instead of" lines first, then trim the steps to one line each.
- Captions burned in (Inter bold, white on a navy #13293D block, lower third), plus a `.vtt` for Wistia.
- Mastered to -14 LUFS (`produce.sh`).

**Build it in the motion studio** (`Content/motion-studio`, talking-head flow):
1. New project `projects/bizxcel-vsl/`. Footage into `footage/`, then transcribe and pick takes into `cut.json`.
2. Restyle `lib/uflow.js` tokens for this project: navy #13293D, gold #B4813C, cream #F7F4EE, Lora for numbers and titles, Inter for captions.
3. Motion inserts, all real figures:
   - `R250 to R16,000 a month` with `SARS, 2026` under it.
   - `Up to 35 months`.
   - If the VSL B maths line was filmed (R500 x 12 = R6,000): **leave it out until Wayne confirms the penalty bands**, or replace it with the R250 x 12 = R3,000 version from Ad 1.
   - Credentials: `SAIPA member firm`, `Registered SARS Tax Practitioner`, `Since 2014`.
   - The five steps as cards, lit in turn.
   - An arrow down + `Fill in the short form below` for the CTA.
4. **Testimonial slot:** leave a 12-second gap marker after the proof line. Drop the testimonial cards in once the calls are done. If they're not ready by launch, cut the "Here's what some of our clients say" line and ship v1 without it.
5. Run the contact-sheet review loop from `motion-studio/CLAUDE.md` before the full render.

**Upload to Wistia:**
- Name: `BX-SARS-VSL-v1`. Thumbnail: a frame of Wayne mid-sentence, not a blank frame.
- Domain restriction: `bizxcelkr.com` and `www.bizxcelkr.com` (plus `localhost` while testing).
- Turn off the end screen, social bar and "more videos".
- Upload the `.vtt` captions.
- Paste the media ID into `SARS_VSL_ID` in `src/app/sars/page.tsx`. A new upload later becomes `sars_vsl_v2` in `VSL_TRACK_AS`.

**Testimonial cards:** make them from the call recordings (spec in the first brief, section 7.3). Upload each as `BX-SARS-testimonial-<business>-<area>`, then add the IDs to `testimonials` in `src/lib/sars-content.ts`. Business type and area only, no surnames, and only with written consent.
